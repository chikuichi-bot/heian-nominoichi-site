車でお越しの場合

名神高速道路「京都東インター」より、三条通を西へ進み、「神宮道」交差点を北へ右折すぐ（東インターより約20分）。

平安神宮境内に駐車場はありません。近隣の有料駐車場をご利用ください（混雑しやすいため、公共交通を推奨します）。

近隣駐車場のご案内

会場周辺の駐車場は混雑しやすく、満車になることがあります。できるだけ公共交通機関でのご来場をおすすめします。

みやこめっせ駐車場は、土曜・日曜・祝日は上限料金がありません（時間課金のみ）。

Access to Okazaki Park

Venue: Okazaki Park (in front of Heian-jingu Shrine).

Recommended Bus Train Car Parking

Recommended routes

1From JR Kyoto Station

City Bus 5 (stand A2) or Raku Bus 100 (stand D1) → get off at “Okazaki Koen / Bijutsukan, Heian Jingu-mae” → walk north about 5 minutes

About 30 min230 yen

2From Hankyu Kyoto-Kawaramachi

City Bus 5, 32 or 46 → “Okazaki Koen / Bijutsukan, Heian Jingu-mae” or “Okazaki Koen / Rohm Theatre Kyoto, Miyakomesse-mae” → walk north about 5 minutes

About 10–15 min by bus

By bus

JR Kyoto Station

City Bus 5 (A2) or Raku Bus 100 (D1) to “Okazaki Koen / Bijutsukan, Heian Jingu-mae”, then walk north 5 minutes. About 30 minutes / 230 yen. About eight buses per hour.

Hankyu Kyoto-Kawaramachi

From Shijo Kawaramachi, City Bus 5 or 32 (stand H) or 46 (stand E). Get off at Okazaki Koen / Bijutsukan, Heian Jingu-mae or Rohm Theatre / Miyakomesse-mae, then walk north 5 minutes.

Gion / Kiyomizu area

City Bus 201, 203 or 206 to Higashiyama Nijo (walk east 5 minutes), or Raku Bus 100 to Okazaki Koen / Bijutsukan, Heian Jingu-mae (walk north 5 minutes).

By train

  • About 10 minutes on foot from Higashiyama Station (T10), Subway Tōzai Line
  • About 15 minutes on foot from Sanjō (40) or Jingu-marutamachi (41), Keihan Oto Line

By car

From Kyoto-higashi IC on the Meishin Expressway, drive west on Sanjō-dōri about 20 minutes, then turn right (north) at Sanjo-jingumichi. No parking inside Heian-jingu; use nearby paid lots (often full—public transport recommended).

Parking nearby

Lots fill up quickly. Please use public transportation if you can.

Miyako Messe has no daily maximum on Sat/Sun/holidays (time-based fees only).
