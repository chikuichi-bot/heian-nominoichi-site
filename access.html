Access to Okazaki Park

Recommended routes

1. From JR Kyoto Station
Take City Bus 5 (stand A2) or Raku Bus 100 (stand D1) to "Okazaki Koen / Bijutsukan, Heian Jingu-mae", then walk north about 5 minutes.
(About 30 minutes / 230 yen)

2. From Hankyu Kyoto-Kawaramachi
Take City Bus 5, 32 or 46 to "Okazaki Koen / Bijutsukan, Heian Jingu-mae" or "Okazaki Koen / Rohm Theatre Kyoto, Miyakomesse-mae", then walk north about 5 minutes.
(About 10–15 minutes by bus)

For other routes and stop details, see below.


◯By Car:

From the Kyoto-higashi Interchange on the Meishin Expressway, drive westward along Sanjō-dōri street for about 20 minutes, and turn right (northward) at the Sanjo-jingumichi intersection. The market will be found not far from here along the Jingu-michi road.

* Parking

Please note that no parking is available inside Heian-jingu Shrine. There are a few toll parkings in the vicinity of Okazaki Park, such as the municipal Okazaki Park Parking Lot (075-761-9617) or the toll parking of Miyako Messe.

However, we recommend that you use public transport or taxis, since parking space may be very limited depending on the season.


◯Nearest bus stops for access by bus:

- JR Kyoto Station to Heian-jingu shrine

Take City Bus 5 departing from stand A2 or Raku Bus 100 from stand D1.

Get off at "Okazaki Koen / Bijutsukan,Heian Jingu-mae" and walk north for five minutes.

The bus will bring you to the park from Kyoto Station in around 30 minutes for a fee of 230 yen. Eight buses leave each hour.


- Hankyu Kyoto-Kawaramachi Station to Heian-jingu shrine

From Shijo Kawaramachi bus stop, take either City Bus 5 or 32 departing from Bus Stand H or City Bus 46 from Bus Stand E.

Get off at either Okazaki Koen / Bijutsukan,Heian Jingu-mae or Okazaki Koen / Rohm Theatre Kyoto, Miyakomesse-mae (Exhibition Hall) bus stops and walk northward for five minutes.


- Gion and Kiyomizu areas to Heian-jingu shrine

Take City Bus 201, 203 or 206, get off at Higashiyama Nijo, Okazaki Koen-guchi bus stop and walk east for 5 minutes.

Take Raku Bus 100, get off at the Okazaki Koen / Bijutsukan Heian Jingu-mae bus stop and walk north for five minutes.


◯Nearest stations for access by train:

A ten-minute walk from Higashiyama Station (T10) on the Kyoto City Subway Tōzai Line

or

A fifteen-minute walk from Sanjō Station (40) or Jingu-marutamachi Station (41) on the Keihan Railway Oto Line.


Parking lots nearby

* Parking near the venue fills up quickly. Please use public transportation if you can.
* At Miyako Messe, there is no daily maximum on Saturdays, Sundays or holidays (time-based fees only). Please take care.

● Municipal Okazaki Park parking lot (capacity: approx. 500 cars) — Mon–Fri: 1,200 yen / 24 hours from entry; Sat, Sun & holidays: 1,800 yen / 24 hours from entry.

● Miyako Messe parking (capacity: approx. 163 cars) — Weekdays: maximum 1,500 yen. Saturdays, Sundays and holidays: no daily maximum.
